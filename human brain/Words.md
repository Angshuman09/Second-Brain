## ==Vigilance==:
the process of paying close and continuous attention.

## ==Reprehensible==: 
morally wrong and deserving criticism

## ==Ambiguously==: 
in a way that allows for or suggests more than one possible meaning

## ==Province==: 
one of the areas that some countries are divided into with its own local government

## ==incumbent==: 
a person who is currently in an official position

## ==compassion==: 
understanding or pity for somebody who is suffering

## ==Schadenfreude==: 
is a complex emotion defined as the experience of pleasure, joy, or satisfaction that comes from witnessing or learning about the troubles, failures, or suffering of another person

## ==Spite==:
the desire to hurt or annoy somebody

## ==Reconciliation==:
the process of comparing two sets of records to make sure they match perfectly.

## ==Reciprocity==:
a social norm and behavioral principle where people respond to favors, gifts, or kindnesses with equivalent actions.

## ==Flinging==:
to throw somebody/something suddenly and carelessly or with great force

## ==Quagmire==:

## ==Idosyncratic==:

## ==Laden==:

## ==Dichotomized== :
means divided or separated into two distinct, often opposing, parts or classifications

the practice of grouping, combining, or considering distinct items, people, or ideas together into a single, broad category without making fine distinctions

a person who enters a place without permission and often secretly

## ==Ubiquitous==:
seeming to be everywhere or in several places at the same time; very common
