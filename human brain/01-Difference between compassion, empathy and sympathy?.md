
|Term|Meaning|Example|
|---|---|---|
|**Empathy**|Understanding and feeling what another person is experiencing from their perspective.|"I can imagine how painful that must feel for you."|
|**Sympathy**|Feeling concern, sorrow, or pity for someone else's suffering.|"I'm sorry you're going through that."|
|**Compassion**|Recognizing someone's suffering and wanting to help relieve it.|"I'm sorry you're hurting. What can I do to help?"|
### A simple way to remember them

Imagine your friend loses their job:

- **Sympathy**: You stand beside them and say, "That's terrible. I'm sorry."
- **Empathy**: You emotionally connect and think, "I can understand how scared and disappointed you must feel."
- **Compassion**: You connect with their pain and then take action: "Let's update your résumé together and look for openings."
### Another analogy: Someone falls into a hole

- **Sympathy**: "That looks awful down there."
- **Empathy**: "I know what it feels like to be in a hole."
- **Compassion**: "I know it's hard down there, here's a ladder."
### Key differences

- **Sympathy** = _feeling for_ someone.
- **Empathy** = _feeling with_ someone.
- **Compassion** = _feeling with someone and being motivated to help._

Empathy helps us understand people. Sympathy expresses care. Compassion turns care into helpful action.
