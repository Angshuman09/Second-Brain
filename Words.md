**Meaning:** Continuing for a long time or lasting despite difficulties.

**Easy Definition:** Lasting for a long time.

**Synonyms:** lasting, continuing, permanent, persistent

**Example from daily life:** They have an enduring friendship that has lasted since childhood.

**Day-to-day use:** **Medium.**

### Availed

**Meaning:** Helped or benefited; usually used in the phrase **"availed oneself of."**

**Easy Definition:** Made use of something or benefited from it.

**Synonyms:** benefited, used, utilized, took advantage of

**Example from daily life:** She availed herself of the opportunity to learn a new skill.

**Day-to-day use:** **Low.**

> **Common phrase:** “It was of no avail” = **It was useless / it didn't help.**

### Longue

**Meaning:** A French word meaning **"long."** In English, it is usually encountered as part of **chaise longue**.

**Easy Definition:** “Long,” as in the long chair called a _chaise longue_.

**Synonyms:** long _(French)_

**Example from daily life:** He stretched out on the chaise longue and read a book.

**Day-to-day use:** **Very Low** on its own.

> **Chaise longue** = a long upholstered chair designed for reclining.
